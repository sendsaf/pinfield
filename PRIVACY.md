# Pinfield privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Pinfield is a browser extension that changes how Pinterest looks in your own browser. It shows the pins
Pinterest sends you as an endless field you can pan and zoom.

## What Pinfield does with your data

- **It collects nothing and sends nothing to its maker or to anyone else.** There is no server, no analytics,
  no tracking and no advertising.
- **It only talks to Pinterest, as you.** To load more pins, open a pin, search, show your boards, save or
  unsave a pin, Pinfield sends the same requests Pinterest's own page sends, from the Pinterest tab, with
  your existing Pinterest session. Those requests go to Pinterest only. Pinterest's own privacy policy
  applies to them.
- **What it keeps, and where:** a few settings in your browser's local storage on pinterest.com: whether
  Pinfield is on, the background colour, the motion setting, recent searches, pins you chose to hide,
  your last-used board, and which pins you saved through Pinfield (so a second press can unsave them).
  They never leave your computer. Clearing Pinterest's site data, or removing Pinfield, removes them.
- **Images** are loaded from Pinterest's own image servers (i.pinimg.com), as Pinterest itself does.

## Permissions

Pinfield runs only on Pinterest's websites. It asks for no other site access and uses no browser features
beyond running its scripts on those pages.
